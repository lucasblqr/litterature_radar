TEAM'S INTEREST - 60-DAY JOURNAL FEEDS
======================================

Added three aggregate pages under "Team's interest":

1. Health Journal
2. Econ Journal
3. General Science Journal

Each page shows all papers from its configured journal family that were published in the past 60 days.

Important behavior:
- No topic score filter.
- No keyword filter.
- No abstract-only filter.
- No saved-status filter.
- No manual journal filter on these pages.
- Papers are ordered newest first.
- Future-dated publication metadata is excluded.
- Personal save/note controls remain available.

To refresh the database with the newest 60 days of papers, run:
    update_papers.bat

Then launch the app with:
    run_app.bat
