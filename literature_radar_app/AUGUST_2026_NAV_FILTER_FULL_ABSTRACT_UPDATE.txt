Literature Radar update — 11 August 2026

Changes in this build:
- Team's interest now contains only the three aggregate journal pages for journal browsing:
  Health Journal, Econ Journal, General Science Journal.
- Individual journal sections were removed from the sidebar.
- Each aggregate journal page still starts from ALL papers in its journal family published in the last 60 days.
- The standard Filters block is now available on those pages (search, abstract availability, saved status, journal).
- Filters are optional; default selections do not remove papers.
- Paper cards display the full stored abstract instead of truncating it at 1,200 characters.
- Exact-ISSN economics collection from the previous build is retained.

No paper database update is required just to use these interface changes.
