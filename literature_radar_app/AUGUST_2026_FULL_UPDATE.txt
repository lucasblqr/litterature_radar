LITERATURE RADAR — FULL UPDATE (August 2026)

This full project includes:

1. Team's interest pages with no topic/ranking filter:
   - Health Journal — last 60 days
   - Econ Journal — last 60 days
   - General Science Journal — last 60 days

2. Econ journal collection fix:
   - Economics journals use exact Crossref ISSN endpoints when ISSNs are configured.
   - This replaces fuzzy journal-title collection for those journals.
   - Both print and electronic ISSNs are queried and results are deduplicated.
   - Online publication date is preferred before print publication date when available.

3. Update commands:
   - update_papers.bat          -> all configured journals, last 60 days
   - update_econ_60_days.bat    -> Econ journals only, last 60 days
   - run_app.bat                -> launch the app after setup

The Team's interest journal pages apply only journal-family + publication-date restrictions.
They do not apply topic, ranking, keyword, abstract, or saved-status filters.
